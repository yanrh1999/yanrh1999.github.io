Our paper, **"Convex--Concave Quadratic Spectral Filtering for Graph Neural Networks,"** has been accepted at **ECML 2026**.

The work introduces DCQ-GNN, a spectral graph neural network based on a compact bank of adaptive convex--concave quadratic filters with node-adaptive gated fusion.

- [Read the paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24956)
- [View the DCQ-GNN code](https://github.com/yanrh1999/DCQ-GNN)

Co-authors: Jia Cai, Mengzhu Chen, and Haodong Yang.
