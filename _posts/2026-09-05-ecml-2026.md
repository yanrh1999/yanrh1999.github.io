I am pleased to share that our paper, **"Convex--Concave Quadratic Spectral Filtering for Graph Neural Networks,"** has been accepted at **ECML 2026**.

The work introduces a graph neural network based on second-order spectral filters with learned convex--concave curvature control and node-adaptive gated fusion.

- Paper: [arXiv:2606.24956](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24956)
- Code: [DCQ-GNN](https://github.com/yanrh1999/DCQ-GNN)

Co-authors: Jia Cai, Mengzhu Chen, and Haodong Yang.
